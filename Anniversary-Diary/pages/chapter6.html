To My Love

My dearest Baby maa❤️

I don't really know where to begin, because when it comes to you, somehow words never feel enough. But today, on our first anniversary, I want to try. One year ago, we started writing a story that I never knew would become such an important part of my life. And now, when I look back at everything we've shared, I can't help but smile. I remember the conversations, the laughter, the silly moments, the little arguments, the moments when we missed each other, and all those tiny memories that probably seemed ordinary at the time. But those ordinary moments became some of my favorite memories.

Because they were moments with you ❤️

Baby maa, thank you for coming into my life. Thank you for every smile you've given me, every conversation we've shared, every time you've cared about me, and every little moment that made me feel lucky to have you. I know that we aren't perfect. We may misunderstand each other sometimes. We may have disagreements. We may have days when things don't go exactly the way we want. But even on those days, I want us to remember why we started.

I choose you. I chose you then, I choose you today, and I will keep choosing you. ❤️

I want to be there for your happiest moments. I want to celebrate your achievements, listen to your dreams, support you when things get difficult, and be the person you can always come back to. I want to make more memories with you. More photographs. More adventures. More late-night conversations. More laughter. More silly moments. More days where we do absolutely nothing and somehow still have the best time. I don't know exactly what the future has planned for us. But if I get to choose one thing about that future, I would choose to have you in it.

You are not just a chapter in my story. You are one of the most beautiful reasons I want to keep writing it. ❤️

So today, on our first anniversary, I just want you to know one simple thing:

I love you, Baby maa.

More than I can properly explain, more than these words can hold, and in all the little ways that I hope you continue to notice. Thank you for being you. Thank you for being part of my life. And thank you for giving me a story worth remembering forever.

With all my love,

Baby ❤️ Maa
One year together. A thousand memories. And hopefully, a lifetime more to come. ❤️
